Hellow fellow worldenators...

If you've been following our devlogs over the past couple of years, you've probably noticed a recurring theme: we set an ambitious deadline, miss it, pivot everything, cancel the game, and then casually show up months later as if nothing happened. 

Which brings us to our latest entry in a long list of backtracking decisions: nine months ago, we literally released a blog post called "Scratch Sucks", declared the Scratch version of Hellow World dead, and said its funeral wasn't even worth attending. Fast-forward through those months of complete silence, and... we're back working on the Scratch version. Again.

Scratch is great, actually. We lied.

Echoless here: 

Jokes aside, I want to be completely honest. Since 2023, I've missed just about every goal, release date, and target I've shared publicly (and privately, too), and have repeatedly reversed "final" decisions.
I know how exhausting that is for anyone trying to follow this project. You get excited about an announcement, like the Steam release date, only to watch it never happen. Managing development, scope creep, burnout, and technical skill has a steep learning curve, and my ambition regularly outpaced reality. I am genuinely sorry for dragging everyone on this rollercoaster.

Here is our honest view of where Hellow World sits right now:

- The Godot version was reset again. It was nearly entirely vibecoded, and we aren't going to pretend otherwise. Early on, leaning on AI felt like an easy way to get stuff done quicker. But as always, when AI writes massive chunks of your code for you, you never actually learn how those systems work, or learn much of anything at all. Rather than spending months nuking the third-party thinker code, we decided it was just best to start fresh. No more vibecoding.

- The Scratch version is the primary version again. We're "actively" developing it; we're releasing snapshots, and it's where most of the current work is happening.

- No more dates, timelines, or promises. We're done setting public goals. No Steam release windows, no targets. Updates will drop when they are ready.

Also, quick side note: we've finally fixed our versioning scheme so that it makes more sense. We've retroactively updated every release to strictly follow MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH, so what was previously known as a0.3.11 is now Alpha 3.1.1, 0.4.0 is Alpha 4.0.0, and the upcoming update is officially Alpha 5.0.0.
Thank you to everyone who has stuck with us through the chaos. Your patience means far more than you think. ❤️
